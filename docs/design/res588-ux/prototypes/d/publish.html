Countermovement jump height across four testing occasions

Sprint priming longitudinal study · CMJ longitudinal research · 2026-11-05

1 · Question and design

Does countermovement jump height change across four repeated testing occasions in the admitted sprint-priming cohort? Observational longitudinal repeated measures; single-arm; 21 athletes.

2 · Methods and estimand

MixedLM random intercept + random occasion slope, ML, Nelder–Mead after BFGS failed. Estimand: within-athlete change in CMJ height per testing occasion. Plan PLAN-7103044E frozen locally; not externally preregistered.

3 · Primary result

+2.995 cm [1.866, 4.124] from occasion 1 to 4; slope +0.998 cm/occasion [0.622, 1.375]; 82 observations in 21 athletes; 2 missing occasion-4 rows.

4 · Limitations

Single-arm observational data; no causal intervention effect. Occasions are deposited testing blocks, not calendar weeks. Two missing occasion-4 rows are not imputed. No raw force traces; CMJ height cannot be independently recomputed.
