Missingness

2 of 82 rows missing. Occasion 4 has 19 athletes. Complete-case analysis; not imputed.

Assumptions

Linear occasion effect; normal athlete intercepts and slopes; missing at random conditional on athlete. The four COND values are ordered testing occasions, not randomised conditions.

What could move the answer

Missing occasion-4 rows. Two athletes are absent at occasion 4. The estimate could move if their change differs systematically; no imputation is used.

Design limits

No control arm. The deposit is single-arm. No causal priming effect is identified.

Occasions ≠ weeks. COND 1–4 are deposited testing blocks; no calendar mapping is claimed.

No raw force traces. CMJ height cannot be independently recomputed.

Practical threshold

No SWC prespecified. The 3 cm change is statistically supported but not automatically practically meaningful. Define a smallest worthwhile change before the next block.
