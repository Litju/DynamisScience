Decision impact

The answer, in context

Statistical signal · not causal
+2.995 cm from occasion 1 to 4 (95% CI 1.866 – 4.124); slope +0.998 cm/occasion.

What this supports

A consistent cohort signal. The confidence interval excludes zero; 16 of 19 complete-pair athletes rose.

What it does not support

No causal priming effect. No control arm.

No practical threshold. No SWC was prespecified.

No calendar claim. Occasions are not weeks.

Next action

Continue the weekly monitoring; follow up the two missing athletes; pre-specify a smallest worthwhile change before the next block.
