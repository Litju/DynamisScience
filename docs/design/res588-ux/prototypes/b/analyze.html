Question. Does countermovement jump height change across four repeated testing occasions in the admitted sprint-priming cohort? Estimand: within-subject change in CMJ height per testing occasion; single-arm and observational.

Design. Observational longitudinal repeated measures. Four deposited testing occasions, not calendar weeks. Population: 21 highly trained male collegiate athletes.

08

Interpretation

scientist-authored note

Countermovement jump height was higher at later deposited testing occasions: the random-slope model estimates +0.998 cm per occasion (95% CI 0.622 to 1.375), or +2.995 cm from occasion 1 to 4 (95% CI 1.866 to 4.124).

This is a within-athlete observational change. The deposit does not map occasions to calendar weeks and contains no raw force traces, so the result cannot be read as a causal priming effect or as season-long monitoring.
