Sprint priming and countermovement-jump height over 24 hours

Data: Holmberg et al. (2025), DOI 10.17632/6p99569x66.1, CC BY 4.0 · 21 athletes · method TREND 1.1.0 (qualified)

Across four occasions from before priming to 24 hours after, CMJ height increased by an estimated 0.998 cm per occasion (95% Wald CI 0.622 to 1.375) EVID-U-0002.

Figure 1. Change from Pre implied by the linear trend, with 95% Wald intervals. Modeled values.

Limitations. Intervals are Wald-type and may be anti-conservative with 21 athletes. The residual normality check is exploratory. Normality of athlete baselines was not formally assessed. The findings apply to this sample only.
